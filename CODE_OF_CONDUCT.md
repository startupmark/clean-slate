# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not about the person.

This is a small project. What it needs is people behaving reasonably and a clear
route to act when someone does not.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect, whatever their experience, background or identity.
- Give feedback about the code and the reasoning, not about the contributor.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. The reason will be given, and
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) states the hard constraints up front so
  nobody wastes their time.
- Be patient. Reviews are done part-time and may take a while.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, commits, and
anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report privately to **cs-oss@triberoi.com**, or use the **Report content**
option on the offending comment.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will respond as promptly as a
part-time project allows, and may warn, remove content or block an account. Since
the maintainer is currently the only person with that authority, a report
concerning the maintainer is better raised with
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
