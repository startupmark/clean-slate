Your data

Everything is stored on this machine. What is stored.

Export writes your settings and everything the filter has learned to a file. Import reads one back and replaces both. That is how you move to a new machine, or come back after a reinstall.

The log of hidden posts stays out of the file. It holds other people’s writing, and that belongs on the machine where it was read.

Unsaved changes. Import, the resets and “Forget everything learned” happen straight away. Everything else waits for Save.
