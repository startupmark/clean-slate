Help

Clean Slate scores every post in your LinkedIn home feed against rules you set, and hides the ones that fall short. Nothing is deleted, and every decision shows its reason.

Getting started

  • Set your topics. In Preferences, add specific terms you want more of. "Developer tools" beats "technology".
  • Stay on Discover for a few days. It is the balanced setting.
  • Use the buttons on hidden posts. They teach the filter. It stays quiet until about eight judgements.

Reading modes

  • Focus. Only high-confidence matches.
  • Discover. The default. Obvious noise is hidden, borderline posts dim.
  • Digest. Only the day's strongest posts. The rest collapse behind one card.
  • Raw. LinkedIn untouched.

If your feed looks empty, check the mode. Focus and Digest are the strict ones.

Scoring

Every post starts at 50. A promoted post loses 100. A muted phrase costs 24. One of your topics is worth 16. A first-degree connection adds 10. A poll takes 20.

Select the verdict label above any post for the exact arithmetic. On a hidden post, use Why?.

What the filter learns from you moves a post by at most 18 points, so your own rules always win.

Buttons

  • Show post. Reveals this one post, and counts as a small vote in favour.
  • More like this. Teaches the filter. Changes no list.
  • Less like this. Teaches the filter the other way. Changes no list.
  • Never miss this author. Their posts stay visible whatever else matches. Does not fade.
  • ★ does the same as Never miss, and toggles off.
  • Hide. Hides one post without judging the author.
  • Mute. Hides every post from that person. The only button that acts on someone rather than a post. Fades after a month unless you repeat it.
  • Why? Opens the arithmetic without opening the post.

Anything that changes a people list offers an Undo for a few seconds.

★, Hide and Mute sit at the top of each post. They are faint until you point at the post, and always visible on a touchscreen.

Fading

A mute fades to nothing over 30 days, then drops off the list. Preferences shows how long each entry has left, and lets you pin it to stop it fading.

Names you type into Preferences never fade. Neither does "Never miss this author". Change the 30 days, or switch fading off, under Filter strength.

Profiles

A profile carries its own topics, muted phrases and Discover threshold. Your people lists, page cleanup and everything the filter has learned are shared across all of them.

Review

Every post it hid, with the reason. Judge each one: Good call or Shouldn't have. Both teach the filter, and the tally at the top shows how often you agreed.

Switch the range to see today, the last seven days, or everything. The log keeps the 200 most recent hidden posts.

If a post was hidden because you muted its author, the row offers to unmute them. Verdicts alone will not lift a mute.

Pausing

Pause on LinkedIn in the toolbar popup stops the filtering. The feed comes back exactly as LinkedIn sends it. Raw mode does the same but keeps your settings live. Nothing is deleted either way.

Export and import

Export writes your settings and everything the filter has learned to a file. Import reads one back and replaces both. Use it to move to a new machine or recover after a reinstall.

The log of hidden posts stays out of the file. It holds other people's writing.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing is being filtered. Refresh the LinkedIn tab. Chrome only injects the extension on navigation.
  • Still nothing. Open the toolbar popup. "Connected · N feed items found" means it is running. Zero items means LinkedIn changed its markup, and a banner will offer a diagnostic and a way to report it.
  • Too much is being hidden. Try Discover, lower the threshold under Filter strength, or forget any learned word that looks wrong.
  • Too little is being hidden. Add muted phrases, and use Hide and Mute for a few days.
  • My feed is not in English. Clean Slate reads English feeds only for now. Scoring still runs, but the rules that read words — promoted posts, polls, engagement bait, “likes this” — match nothing, so more gets through. Other languages are a later pass.
  • Where did my sidebar go? Clean Slate hides the right-hand rail and some left sidebar extras. Turn either off under Page cleanup in Preferences.
  • It is paused and I forgot. Paused and broken look the same from the feed. The toolbar popup says which, and so does the corner of the page.
